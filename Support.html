Where is my data stored?

All CTRON data is stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload or sync it to any server, so uninstalling the app will remove your records. Use iCloud Backup if you want a safety copy on Apple's terms.

How do I back up my records?

CTRON relies on standard iOS / iCloud Backup. Enable iCloud Backup in Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup to keep a copy of your device, including CTRON's data.

Can I import transactions from my bank?

CTRON is intentionally local-only and does not connect to banks or brokerages. You can add transactions manually or import a file from another app you trust.

How do prices update in the Investments tab?

Prices in CTRON are entered manually so you stay in control. There is no live market feed — CTRON is an information and tracking tool, not a brokerage.

Why was my camera/photos permission denied?

You can re-enable Camera and Photos access for CTRON at any time in iOS Settings → CTRON. Receipt capture is optional, and CTRON works without those permissions.

Does CTRON provide investment advice?

No. CTRON is an information and tracking tool. It does not place trades, recommend securities, or predict prices. Always consult a qualified professional before making investment decisions.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside CTRON and choose Reset, or simply uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our servers, deletion is instant and complete.
