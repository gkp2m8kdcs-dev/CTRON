Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

In one sentence

CTRON stores all of your financial data locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal information with anyone — because we never receive it in the first place.

1. Overview

CTRON ("the App", "we", "us") is a local-first iPhone application for tracking your personal finances, including accounts, transactions, budgets, savings goals, investments, and receipts. This Privacy Policy explains what information CTRON does and does not collect, how it is stored, and the limited circumstances under which any data leaves your device.

The short version: your data never leaves your phone, and we have built CTRON so that we cannot see it either.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CTRON does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, address, phone number).
  • Account credentials or sign-in data.
  • Financial account numbers, brokerage credentials, or bank logins.
  • Transaction details, balances, holdings, or cost basis data.
  • Receipt photos, statements, or other attachments.
  • Location information.
  • Device identifiers used to track you across apps or services.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All records you create in CTRON — including transactions, accounts, budgets, savings goals, investment holdings, prices you enter, notes, and receipt images — are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard on-device storage. This data is controlled by iOS and protected by the device's passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID settings.

Because this data never reaches our servers, we cannot recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or wipe your device without backing up, your records will be deleted along with the app.

4. iOS Permissions

CTRON requests only the iOS permissions it needs to work, and only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — used only when you choose to capture a receipt or statement photo to attach to a transaction. Photos are saved inside the app, not to your camera roll unless you choose to export a report image.
  • Photo Library — used only when you choose to attach an existing photo from your library to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (Add) — used only when you export a financial report image to your photo library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Backups

Standard iOS and iCloud backups may include CTRON's local data if you have an iCloud Backup enabled on your device. These backups are governed by Apple's iCloud terms and your Apple ID settings. CTRON does not create or manage its own cloud backup.

6. Third-Party Services

CTRON does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs. There is no behavioral advertising and no data brokerage.

Apple may collect anonymized, aggregated App Store usage data and crash diagnostics under Apple's own privacy policy. CTRON does not control that data; please review Apple's policies for details.

7. Children

CTRON is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age required by your country's law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

8. Your Choices and Controls

Because CTRON stores everything locally, you are always in control of your data. You can:

  • Edit or delete any transaction, account, goal, budget, or holding at any time within the app.
  • Remove all app data by uninstalling CTRON or by using the in-app reset option (if available).
  • Manage permissions for the camera and photo library through iOS Settings.
  • Disable iCloud Backup of CTRON data by managing iCloud Backup settings on your device.

9. International Users

CTRON is offered worldwide through the App Store. Because we do not collect personal information, data-protection laws such as the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, and similar frameworks apply primarily to the data we never receive. If you have questions about your rights, you can contact us at the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Because CTRON does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly; please review this page periodically.

11. No Investment Advice

CTRON is an information and tracking tool. It does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice, does not place trades, and does not predict prices. Always consult a qualified professional before making investment decisions. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
